# Digit entry views

A digit entry view fills the screen and prompts a series of digits, such as a PIN, through a digit-specific keyboard. An optional title and prompt appear above the digit line; use them to explain why digits are needed.

Always use secure digit fields for sensitive data; they display asterisks instead of entered digits onscreen.

Supported on tvOS; not supported in iOS, iPadOS, macOS, visionOS, or watchOS.

Resources: [Virtual keyboards](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/virtual-keyboards), [`TVDigitEntryViewController`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/tvuikit/tvdigitentryviewcontroller) — TVUIKit, a view controller that enables digit entry such as a passcode.

Source: [Apple HIG — Digit entry views](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/digit-entry-views), captured 2026-09-12.
