# Live-viewing apps

Prioritize live content on every screen and make it distinguishable from video-on-demand (VOD) at a glance. Minimize the time from launch to playback; put live content in the first tab when possible so viewing takes one tap or fewer. A Watch Now button can disappear as playback begins, replacing the app UI with full-screen immersive viewing.

## Best practices

- Make live content visibly live. Playing it is best, and you can also mark it with a badge, symbol, or sash, such as in a “Live” channel row. Consider a progress bar showing how much in-progress content remains.
- Keep playback primary while offering supported actions (record, restart, download, favorite) in a consistent order such as Watch, Start Over, Record, Favorite. Show other scheduled airings so people can plan viewing.
- Consider a content footer for channel browsing during playback. Give it a subtle treatment, such as darkening, to keep text legible and all items visually distinct from the content behind it; identify the current thumbnail (for example, badge it or tint its progress bar); use the same categories as the EPG; and make invocation/dismissal predictable (for example, swipe up/down).
- Give instant visual feedback on channel changes: it confirms the destination and gives the stream time to load.
- Match the playing live content’s audio even while people browse with it in the background; stop audio when they leave the app’s live tab.

## EPG experience

An electronic program guide (EPG) lists scheduled programming. Make the current program, channel, and time prominent when it opens so people can return to the current channel immediately. Make dense browsing easy with paging, scrolling, or jumping; consider My Channels or Favorites. Group content into familiar categories (Movies, TV Shows, Kids, Sports, Popular). Let people browse without leaving current content, for example by continuing playback in Picture in Picture or the background.

## Cloud DVR

If you support cloud digital video recording (DVR):

- Let people start and stop recording from the live-stream info panel.
- From a detailed future-program view, offer recording of that program or all future episodes.
- Let people specify recording scope, such as the current episode, new episodes only, or games involving particular teams.
- In the cloud DVR detail view, allow playback, deletion, and (when applicable) recording-setting changes.
- Consider cloud DVR controls for deleting watched or older recordings and automatic storage management that overwrites the oldest or already viewed content to prevent running out of space.

## Platform considerations

No additional considerations for iOS, iPadOS, macOS, tvOS, visionOS, or watchOS.
