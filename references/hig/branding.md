# Branding

Express a recognizable brand identity while feeling at home on the platform and giving people a consistent experience. Brand expression can appear in the app icon, throughout the experience, and in App Store presentation; see the [App Store Marketing Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/).

## Best practices

- Use the brand’s distinctive voice and tone in all written communication. An encouraging, optimistic voice might use plain words, occasional exclamation marks or emoji, and simple sentences.
- Apply accent color judiciously. Broad use can overwhelm the interface and dilute impact. Minimize it on controls; use it intentionally for primary actions or status, such as unread badges or a selected tab icon. Consider placing brand color in the content layer, which scrolls beneath Liquid Glass controls and is picked up dynamically.
- Consider a custom font when the brand is strongly associated with it. Ensure legibility at every size and support Bold Text and Dynamic Type. Custom type can work well for headlines and subheadings while system fonts handle body copy and captions, where small-size legibility matters most.
- Express the brand with familiar components. If customizing appearance, preserve platform-appropriate sizing, placement, and behavior so the experience remains reliable and familiar.
- Defer branding to content. Use refined, unobtrusive brand elements that do not displace or distract from the content people care about.
- Use standard patterns consistently: expected UI locations, standard symbols for common actions, and established navigation and modality conventions help people feel comfortable.
- Resist displaying the logo throughout the app or game unless it provides essential context; use the space for useful information and controls.
- Avoid using a launch screen as a branding opportunity. It disappears too quickly to convey information; consider a branded welcome or onboarding screen at the beginning instead.
- Follow Apple’s trademark guidelines. Apple trademarks must not appear in an app name or images; consult the [Apple Trademark List](https://www.apple.com/legal/intellectual-property/trademark/appletmlist.html) and [Guidelines for Using Apple Trademarks](https://www.apple.com/legal/intellectual-property/guidelinesfor3rdparties.html).

Platform considerations: no additional considerations for iOS, iPadOS, macOS, tvOS, visionOS, or watchOS.
