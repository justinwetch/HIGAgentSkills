# Designing for iPadOS

iPad provides a large, mobile display for media, games, productivity, and creation. It may be held, rested, or placed on a stand; viewing distance is typically within about 3 feet.

## Device characteristics

- **Display:** large, high-resolution.
- **Inputs:** Multi-Touch/virtual keyboards, attached keyboard or pointing device, Apple Pencil, and voice; modes are often combined.
- **App interactions:** quick actions can expand into hours of work, play, media, or creation; people view multiple apps together and use drag and drop between them.
- **System features:** Multitasking, widgets, and drag and drop.

## Best practices

- Elevate important content on the large display; minimize modal interfaces and full-screen transitions, keeping controls reachable without obscuring content.
- Choose content size and density according to viewing distance and input mode.
- Support Multi-Touch, physical keyboard/trackpad, and Apple Pencil; consider combined-input interactions.
- Adapt to orientation, multitasking modes, Dark Mode, and Dynamic Type, including transition to macOS.

Source: [Apple HIG — Designing for iPadOS](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-ipados), captured 2026-09-12.
